// 01 — The ask

The ask

Calibrate Earth transforms the lives of tomorrow’s leaders from under-resourced communities — immersive spaces for personal growth, and the social-emotional and professional skills to reinvest those talents back home. Their flagship Connections® program, paid training and alumni placement are the bedrock of that impact.

They needed a site that could articulate the mission, showcase the programs, and work as a real hub for fundraising.

// 02 — The delivery

The delivery

As part of my annual commitment to pro-bono work, I built the site from the elegant design they provided — every page, every detail matched to their brand.

The key piece was a custom donation widget. I engineered it to sit inside their existing theming and cover the full range of their fundraising: one-time or recurring gifts, custom payment tiers, and a giving flow that stays out of the donor’s way.
